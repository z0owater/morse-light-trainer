# Changelog

## 1.2.1

- Dark mode. Choose View > Light, Dark or Match system; the choice is saved. Match
  system follows Windows or macOS when you change it, but never while the lamp is
  running. The lamp keeps the light box colours in both modes.
- Windows draws a dark title bar in dark mode, and on a Mac the app uses the
  system's own dark appearance.

## 1.2.0

- First release: CW Player 4.7.5 lamp timing, Part A and Part B exams, full
  recording with Member A and Member B, an answer sheet like the paper one,
  marking at 90%, CW Player text files, and builds for Windows and macOS.
